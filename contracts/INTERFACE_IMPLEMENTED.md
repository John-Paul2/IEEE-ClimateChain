## 2. Summary of Contract Responsibilities

1. **[`DeviceRegistry.sol`](file:///c:/Users/LENOVO/Documents/Hackathons/IEEE%20Climate%20Change/contracts/src/DeviceRegistry.sol)**: Anchors edge gateway identities (`deviceId`) and their 65-byte uncompressed NIST P-256 public keys (`0x04 || X || Y`) to an `installationId` and `operator` address, supporting key rotation and irreversible device revocation.
2. **[`BondVault.sol`](file:///c:/Users/LENOVO/Documents/Hackathons/IEEE%20Climate%20Change/contracts/src/BondVault.sol)**: Holds native ETH/POL operator bonds (per `installationId`) and verifier stakes (per verifier `address`), enforces mandatory withdrawal delays (`180s` demo / `604800s` real), freezes operator withdrawals while a challenge is open, and distributes slashed funds via a `ReentrancyGuard`-protected pull-payment pattern (`claimRewards`).
3. **[`BatchAnchor.sol`](file:///c:/Users/LENOVO/Documents/Hackathons/IEEE%20Climate%20Change/contracts/src/BatchAnchor.sol)**: Records 32-byte `keccak256` batch Merkle roots from active, sufficiently bonded devices, enforces gapless and strictly increasing packet sequence ranges (`firstSeq..lastSeq`), and verifies Merkle inclusion proofs (`verifyPacketInclusion`) using OpenZeppelin's `MerkleProof`.
4. **[`VerifierQuorum.sol`](file:///c:/Users/LENOVO/Documents/Hackathons/IEEE%20Climate%20Change/contracts/src/VerifierQuorum.sol)**: Coordinates the 5-verifier committee through two-phase commit-reveal voting (`keccak256(abi.encode(batchId, modelHash, inputHash, scoreBp, uint8(verdict), salt))`), finalizes batches upon reaching 3-of-5 consensus (`Verified` or `Quarantined`), and slashes verifiers who commit without revealing.
5. **[`Challenge.sol`](file:///c:/Users/LENOVO/Documents/Hackathons/IEEE%20Climate%20Change/contracts/src/Challenge.sol)**: Opens a configurable dispute window (`120s` demo / `86400s` real) for quarantined batches, verifies operator counter-evidence and Merkle proofs, and executes 2-of-3 `ARBITER_ROLE` council resolutions (either upholding fraud and slashing 50% of the operator bond, or overturning the flag, clearing the batch, and slashing 10% of the flagging verifiers' stake to compensate the operator).
6. **[`Attestation.sol`](file:///c:/Users/LENOVO/Documents/Hackathons/IEEE%20Climate%20Change/contracts/src/Attestation.sol)**: Issues immutable, non-transferable period-end CBAM-aligned attestations after checking that all included batches belong to the installation and have reached `Verified` or `Cleared` finality.
